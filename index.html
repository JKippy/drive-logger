        Help & voice commands

        Tap the orb and allow the microphone and location. Say “log …” or “intervention …”, then describe the event. It saves after about 2.5 s of silence, timestamped and located at the moment you said the keyword. Copy puts the session on the clipboard for Sheets or Excel: Date · Time · ISO timestamp · Type · Note · Lat · Lon (Lat/Lon only while GPS is on). Priority stays in the note as $$$ / $$ / $.

        Priority“mark that high priority” · “make it medium” · “that’s low priority” · “bump that to high” · “that’s urgent” · “give the one before that high priority” · “remove the priority”. While dictating: “log high priority brake fade…”
        Delete“scratch that” · “delete that last one” · “never mind” (right after a note)
        Undo“bring that back” · “undo”
        Add“add to that, …” (any time) · “also, …” (within 60 s)
        Type“change that to an intervention” · “make that a log”
        Mark“mark” saves a timestamped [marked event]. Fill it in later with “add to that, …”
        Ask“read that back” · “how many notes” · “how many interventions”
        Pause“pause notes” → only “resume notes” is heard

        Timestamps: each note uses this phone's clock (shown as Now above the notes) at the moment speech recognition first showed the keyword, or the start of the utterance if it opens with the keyword. Recognition usually reports words about 0.5–1.5 s after you say them, so times can run that much late. Drive is time since the session started; GPS ±5 m is location accuracy.

        Accuracy: start each note with the keyword after a short pause. Near-misses like “blog”, “lock” or “in convention” are accepted at the start. Add anything it keeps mishearing (names, places, jargon) under Corrections.

        Say commands as their own sentence. They're never taken from inside a note. The app stops listening while it speaks a reply.

        iPhone: keep the app in the foreground with the screen on. iOS stops listening on lock or app switch and resumes when you return. Safari may ask for mic and location again after a reload. Needs Siri & Dictation enabled. Turn off Silent mode to hear replies. Install with Share › Add to Home Screen; if listening misbehaves there, use a Safari tab. Notes are stored only on this phone (saved instantly to two separate stores), so export after each drive. Don't use Private Browsing: it erases notes when the tab closes.
